# Conventions

## Editing rules

- Always invoke `seaforged-brand` skill when touching visuals
- Product descriptions: 1-2 sentences max, benefit-oriented
- Keep logo and badges at the top
- Product table columns: Product | Type | Status | Repo
- Identity lines (veteran-owned, NDAA, Part 107, Made in USA) stay intact

## Verify Checklist

- [ ] Logo and badges still at top
- [ ] Product table renders correctly in GitHub preview
- [ ] Every product row has Type and Status
- [ ] Identity lines preserved
- [ ] Brand colors unchanged (use brand skill to verify)
